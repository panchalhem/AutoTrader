# Path to $2,000/week — Platform Engineer (AWS, EMR, Spark, Snowflake)

## The math
$2,000/week over 10-20 hrs = **$100-200/hour effective rate**. Senior data/
platform engineers with AWS + EMR + Spark + Snowflake routinely bill
$120-180 AUD/hr on contract in Australia (or higher on international
platforms in USD). At $150/hr, 13-14 hrs/week hits the target — well inside
your 10-20 hr budget. This is realistic, not optimistic, PROVIDED you target
the right channels — generic gig platforms (basic Upwork/Fiverr postings)
will not get you there; specialized/vetted platforms and direct outreach will.

## Why this skillset specifically is in demand right now
- Companies are actively migrating OFF legacy Hadoop/EMR onto Snowflake —
  that migration expertise (EMR/Spark -> Snowflake) is a well-defined,
  urgently-needed, high-value project type.
- Snowflake compute costs are a constant pain point — "Snowflake cost
  optimization" is a concrete, sellable, outcome-based service (you can
  point to a % cost reduction as your pitch, not just hours worked).
- AWS data platform build-outs (EMR clusters, data lakes, pipeline
  orchestration) remain in steady demand across mid-size and enterprise
  companies who can't justify a full-time senior hire.

## Recommended channels (in priority order)

### 1. Fractional / part-time contract work — best fit for your hours
Many companies can't afford a full-time senior platform engineer but need
10-20 hrs/week of expert guidance on their data platform. This is
increasingly a recognized engagement model ("fractional data engineer" /
"fractional platform engineer"), and it matches your availability exactly.
- Search LinkedIn for "fractional data engineer", "part-time Snowflake
  consultant", "contract EMR engineer".
- Specialized staffing agencies for data roles: Paxus, Peoplebank, Talent
  International (AU-focused); also check Toptal and Braintrust below.

### 2. Vetted freelance platforms (not generic gig marketplaces)
- **Toptal** — vets for top-tier talent, has a dedicated data engineering
  category, pays well above average-Upwork rates.
- **Braintrust** — talent-owned marketplace, no platform fee cut from your
  rate, active data engineering / AWS demand.
- **Gun.io** — curated senior developer/data engineer marketplace.
- Regular Upwork/Fiverr are worth a try too, but position yourself in the
  "Expert-Vetted" tier and price at your real rate ($120+/hr) rather than
  competing on price — underpricing signals junior, which defeats the goal.

### 3. A specific, sellable offer (better than "hire me as a freelancer")
Outcome-based offers close faster and command higher rates than generic
hourly listings. Two concrete starting pitches:
- **"Snowflake cost audit + optimization"** — fixed-price engagement
  ($2,000-5,000), you find and fix compute/storage waste, easy ROI story
  for the client to justify hiring you.
- **"EMR/Spark to Snowflake migration assessment"** — a paid scoping
  engagement (1-2 weeks) that naturally leads into the full migration
  contract.

### 4. Direct outreach
- LinkedIn: post about your AWS/EMR/Spark/Snowflake experience, comment
  usefully on data engineering discussions, DM CTOs/Heads of Data at
  mid-size companies with a specific, short pitch (the cost-audit offer
  above is a strong opener since it's low-commitment for them).
- Existing network: past colleagues/managers are often the fastest first
  client — a warm referral skips the trust-building freelance platforms
  require.

## What I'd avoid
- Don't compete on generic Upwork "build me a data pipeline" postings at
  low rates — you'll get bid down to $30-50/hr against much less experienced
  freelancers. Position on the specialized platforms/outcome-based offers
  instead.
- Don't rely on trading profits (from the automation system we built this
  week) as part of this income plan — that's a separate, genuinely
  uncertain activity, not a dependable weekly income source.
